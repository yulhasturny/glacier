Rhône Glacier · 2022In a single year, the Rhône Glacier lost 44.7 million cubic metres of ice. Pressed into one block, that would be a cube with edges 355 metres long.

For comparison, at the same scaleZurich’s Prime Tower is 126 metres tall. The Grossmünster reaches about 62 metres to the tips of its towers. In front lies a football pitch, 105 × 68 metres.

45 measured yearsEach block is one year. GLAMOS measured the Rhône Glacier from 1885 to 1909 and from 2007 to 2026. In five years the glacier grew: those blocks are dashed.
